Built-in signs
  • 1 to 4 fingers: One to Four
  • Open hand: Hello
  • Fist: Yes
  • Thumb up: Good
  • Thumb down: No
  • Thumb + little finger: Call me
  • Thumb + index + little: I love you
  • Both palms together: Namaste
  • Both open hands apart: Welcome
  • Both thumbs up: Very good
  • Both fists: Stop
Teach it a sign (one or two hands)

Type the word, press Record, then hold the sign. It counts down 3 seconds, then records for 3 seconds. Record 2 to 3 times, tilting your hand a little each time.

    Lower is stricter. Raise it if your signs are missed, lower it if it guesses wrong.
